# Presentation Outline: Mapping Food Access in Prince George's County
*Seeds of Tomorrow — [library/community partner event]*

1. **Title slide** — project name, your name, Seeds of Tomorrow branding
2. **The problem, locally** — 17% of MD's food-insecure population is
   in PG County; 1 in 7 residents affected; zip 20743 is the most
   food-insecure in the state
3. **What "food access" actually measures** — quick explainer on the
   USDA low-income + low-access flag (1 mi urban / 10 mi rural)
4. **The map** — live or screenshotted choropleth from `make_map.py`,
   walk through 2-3 specific tracts
5. **Chart 1** — how many tracts are flagged, by distance measure
6. **Chart 2** — poverty rate vs. vehicle access (why distance +
   transportation compound each other)
7. **Chart 3** — SNAP participation hot spots
8. **Chart 4** — kids and seniors living in flagged tracts (make it
   personal — these are specific neighborhoods, not abstract stats)
9. **Recommendations** — mobile markets, coordination with the
   county's Food Equity Council, prioritizing 20743-area tracts
10. **Limitations + what's next** — distance isn't the whole story;
    invite the audience/partner to react and correct your read of
    the local situation
11. **Thank you / links** — GitHub repo, contact info

Keep slides text-light — the charts and map do the talking. Bring
printed handouts of the map for a library event where wifi may be
unreliable.
